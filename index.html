Save & Run on Your Phone

This app is 100% self-contained. Your recipes and custom weights stay on your device.

Option A · Offline File (Zero Hosting)

1. Save this recipe-gram-converter.html file to your phone (iPhone Files app or Android Downloads).
2. Tap it anytime to open in Safari or Chrome — it works offline with no account or internet required.
3. Copy any recipe's ingredients from a website or notes app, tap Paste, and weigh!

Option B · Add to Home Screen + Share Sheet

To appear in your phone's Share Sheet or run as a full-screen Home Screen app:
• Host free in 10 seconds: Drop this HTML file onto Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages, then open the URL on your phone and tap Add to Home Screen.
• Share via URL parameter: GramScale automatically reads ?text=... on launch. On iPhone, create a 2-step Apple Shortcut ("Receive Text from Share Sheet" → "Open URL: your-app-url?text=[Shortcut Input]") so sharing any recipe text opens GramScale with everything pre-converted!
